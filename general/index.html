Strategy Basics

Fundamentals every player should know, regardless of clan family.

Attacking

Scout the base before attacking, fully clear the funnel before committing heroes, and always deploy the right spells at the right moment — not all at once.

Clan War League (CWL)

A monthly 7-day league where clans are grouped by strength. Each clan gets one attack per day against a matched clan — consistency across the week matters more than any single war.

Clan Games

A recurring event where members complete challenges (troops trained, spells brewed, loot donated) to earn points toward clan-wide rewards.
