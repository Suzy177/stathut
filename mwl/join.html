Season 9 registration opens September 10

Mark the date — signups across all nine divisions open on September 10. Join the Discord and watch the MWL channel so you don't miss the window.

What you'll need when registration opens

  • ✓ Your clan tag and current name
  • ✓ Which division you're registering for — see the Rules page for all nine and their formats
  • ✓ Your master roster, within that division's account cap (7–75 depending on division)
  • ✓ Two representatives who can negotiate war timing on Discord
  • ✓ Confirmation your clan can commit to the full season's war schedule
