Guide

New to the Mythical War League? Here's how a season runs from start to finish.

Home Standings Rules Guide Join
  1. 1

    Signups open

    Before a new season, a signup window opens on the Join page. Clans apply with their tag and current Town Hall range.

  2. 2

    Clans get grouped

    Applied clans are sorted into a division based on strength, so matches stay competitive rather than one-sided.

  3. 3

    Season kicks off

    The season runs across a fixed number of war cycles — normal prep day / war day pairs, same as a regular Clan War, but each result also updates the season standings.

  4. 4

    Standings update after every war

    Wins, draws and losses add points to a clan's season total. Check the Standings page to see where every clan sits.

  5. 5

    Season wraps up

    At the end of the season, final standings are locked in. Where multiple divisions exist, top clans move up and bottom clans move down for the next season.

MWL hasn't started its first season yet — this guide describes how it's designed to work once it launches. See the Rules page for scoring specifics.
