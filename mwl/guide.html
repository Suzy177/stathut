MWL · Season 9

Guide

New to MWL? Here's how Season 9 runs from registration to season wrap.

Home Standings Rules Guide Join
  1. 1

    Registration opens September 10

    Clans apply for one of the three divisions — Basilisk, Sphinx or Chimera — with their clan tag and master roster.

  2. 2

    Rosters are locked to a division

    Each division has its own account cap (30 for Basilisk, 45 for Sphinx, 60 for Chimera) and Town Hall composition. A player can only sit on one roster per division.

  3. 3

    Clans negotiate war timing

    Each clan names two representatives who agree on a war time in the designated Discord channels, within the Tuesday–Monday 17:00 UTC window (defaulting to Sunday 17:00 UTC if no agreement is reached).

  4. 4

    Standings update after every war

    Results feed into the season table. Check the Standings page to see where every clan sits in its division.

  5. 5

    Season wraps up

    At the end of Season 9, final standings are locked in per division. See the Rules page for scoring and penalty details.
