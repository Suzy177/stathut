Rules

How scoring, seasons and eligibility work in the Mythical War League.

Home Standings Rules Guide Join

🗓️ Season Length

Each MWL season runs for a fixed number of war cycles. War days and prep days follow the same rhythm as a regular Clan War — the difference is that results feed into the season standings instead of standing alone.

🏆 Scoring

A win is worth 3 points, a draw 1 point, and a loss 0 points. Standings are ranked by total points first, then by total stars and destruction percentage as tiebreakers.

✅ Eligibility

Any clan can apply to join a season during the signup window (see the Join page). Clans are grouped so that matchups stay competitive rather than lopsided.

⚖️ Fair Play

No sniping teammates, no intentional sandbagging, and no smurf/rented accounts fielded just for a war. Clans found breaking these standards can be removed from the season and lose their standing.

🔁 Promotion & Relegation

Where multiple divisions are running, the top clans in a division move up next season and the bottom clans move down — keeping each division's competition level roughly even over time.

📣 Disputes

Any scoring or conduct dispute should be raised in the MWL Discord channel before the next war cycle starts, with screenshots/replay evidence where possible.

Note: these rules describe how MWL is designed to run. Exact details may be adjusted before Season 1 officially opens — check back or watch the Discord for the finalized ruleset.
