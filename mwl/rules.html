🧾 Roster Management

Each division has its own master roster cap (see above). A player can only appear on one roster per division. Additions are allowed only during regular season play; deletions are allowed any time as long as the clan stays above the minimum account requirement.

🗓️ Match Scheduling & Disputes

Each clan names two representatives to negotiate war timing in the designated Discord channels. Wars run between Tuesday 17:00 UTC and Monday 17:00 UTC. If the two sides can't agree on a time, it defaults to Sunday 17:00 UTC.

⚠️ Penalties for Violations

Fielding an unrostered player freezes that player's attacks. Failing to send or accept a challenge within the given window results in a forfeit. Streaming the war to the opponent costs a 3-star deduction. Using a banned account carries a minimum 3-month suspension.

⚖️ Fair Play Standards

Modding, base stealing and spying are all prohibited and can result in a permanent ban from the league.

This is a summary of the Season 9 ruleset. For the full, word-for-word rules document, check the MWL Discord.
